# Privacy policy: Terrarium (Focus Debt)

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Terrarium has **no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no tracking**. It loads no remote code.

**What stays in your browser** (`chrome.storage`): the sites you track, your limits, time and item counts for the last 14 days, your plant progress and your settings. If you are signed in to Chrome, non-secret settings sync through Chrome's own sync to your other Chrome browsers. Your API key, if you add one, is never synced.

**Camera (optional, off by default).** If you turn on Focus Mirror or a camera-checked exercise, video is analysed on your device by a bundled model. Frames are never saved, uploaded or shared. Only a few numbers (blink rate, eyes closed, yawns, distance, rep count) are used, and only in memory. Chrome shows its camera indicator while it is on. Turn it off any time in Settings.

**Optional AI tasks (off by default).** If you add a Gemini or OpenAI key, the mood, time and hobby you pick in the Life Mixer are sent to that provider to write a task. Nothing else is sent. Without a key, nothing leaves your browser.

**Page access.** The extension reads the address (host and path) of the feed sites you track, and counts scrolling, only to measure time on feeds. It does not read page content, and it does not run on other sites unless you add them and grant Chrome's permission for that site.
